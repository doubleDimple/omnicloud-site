Terms of Service

Effective date: October 24, 2026

The app

OmniCloud connects to cloud accounts and servers that you own or are authorized to manage. It supports Oracle Cloud, Hetzner Cloud, Google Cloud, Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, netcup and OVHcloud, with different features available for each provider. Cloudflare has a separate workspace. SSH and SFTP tools connect directly to servers you provide.

You choose the accounts, connections and operations. Some features continue after setup, such as credential renewal, enabled iCloud synchronization and certificate renewal on your server. There is no OmniCloud-operated cloud-management backend.

Websites & Certificates installs and manages Nginx on a supported server you provide. The first version supports a fresh server or a recognized OmniCloud installation; it does not take over an existing, independently installed Nginx. Installation and certificate tasks run on your server and can continue after the app disconnects.

Your responsibilities

  • Use OmniCloud only with cloud accounts, projects, domains and servers you are permitted to manage.
  • You are responsible for your cloud providers’ charges, including those for servers, disks, IP addresses and traffic that you create or keep through the app.
  • Keep your device secure. Anyone who can unlock the app can use its connected accounts; consider enabling the app lock.

Prices and estimates

Cloud prices and cost estimates come from providers’ published prices, available billing data or local estimates. Currency conversions use public European Central Bank reference rates when available. Estimates may differ from checkout totals because of taxes, discounts, usage or exchange rates. Your provider’s invoice is authoritative.

Pro and Pro+ subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple. The app shows the App Store price and billing period before purchase; any trial is shown only when Apple reports it is available to you. Subscription charges are separate from cloud-provider and server charges. Removing the app does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Third-party services

Oracle Cloud, Hetzner Cloud, Google Cloud, AWS, Microsoft Azure, netcup, OVHcloud and Cloudflare are independent services governed by their own terms. Apple handles subscriptions and optional iCloud synchronization. Certificate tasks use Let’s Encrypt and Cloudflare from your server. These services and their charges are governed by their respective terms; OmniCloud is not affiliated with or endorsed by the cloud providers.
